Disclosures

How shop links work on Cigar Nexus, and what is independent editorial content.

Affiliate links

Some profiles and the map drawer include a button to shop at Famous Smoke Shop. That link is an affiliate partnership (Commission Junction / CJ). If you buy something after using it, Cigar Nexus may earn a commission. That does not change the price you pay.

Those links use rel="sponsored". A short notice also sits next to the button itself.

The map is not a paid listing

Brands, factories, and people appear because of documented industry relationships, not because someone paid for a node. Cigar Nexus is not a store, not a manufacturer, and not affiliated with Habanos, STG, Altadis, or the houses on the map except as an independent reference.

Age

This site is for adults 21 and over. Tobacco-related content is shown only after that confirmation.
